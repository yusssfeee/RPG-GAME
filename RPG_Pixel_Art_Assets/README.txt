# Tiny Acre — Pixel-art asset pack

This pack contains AI-generated, individually cropped PNG sprites:
- 9 farm animals
- 40 fantasy monsters, variants and story bosses
- Original transparent sprite sheets in `source_sheets/`

All sprites are PNG with transparent backgrounds. They are supplied as art assets only;
they have NOT yet been wired into the RPG HTML file.

## Sprite groups
- `animals/`: chicken, sheep, bee, cow, duck, rabbit, goat, pig, llama
- `monsters/`: common enemies, variants, elite forms and story/cave bosses
